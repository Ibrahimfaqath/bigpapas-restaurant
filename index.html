About The Restaurant

Hearty Dishes for Every Meal

Big Papa's Restaurant is a well-known casual dining spot located at Shakirin Complex in Kiulap, Bandar Seri Begawan.

The restaurant offers a varied selection of local Malaysian specialties, Asian noodle and rice options, and Western grilled favorites tailored for everyday dining with family and friends.

Specialty Offerings

Known for Nasi Lemak, Chicken Chop, Steaks, Lamb Chop, and local hot buns.

Convenient Location

Situated in Block A, Shakirin Complex, Kg. Kiulap.
